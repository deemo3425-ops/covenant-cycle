## Why this changed

Editions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 were published under CC BY-ND 4.0 — NoDerivatives. That was chosen to
protect the signatures: several of this book's apparent errors are deliberate, load-bearing, and
documented in `revision-log.md`. A "corrected" copy is a defaced one, and forbidding modified
versions was the way to keep each teller's hand intact, tremor included.

It also kept the book out of the rooms where books are now read. Collections that gather text —
for study, for translation, for training — filter NoDerivatives out by default, not because the
license forbids them but because it makes them ask a lawyer a question, and the cheap answer to
that question is no. A book written to be answered was being declined unread.

So the author chose the corpus. The trade is real and is not hidden here: under CC BY, anyone may
publish a changed *Covenant Cycle*. The `:wq!` may be deleted. The two straight ellipses may be
curled to match the ten. The two chapters sharing a numeral may be renumbered. That is now
permitted, and the author permitted it knowingly.

Nobody who already holds a copy loses anything. These terms are wider than the ones they received,
never narrower.

## What is asked instead of required

The license no longer forbids correcting this book. The request stands anyway, and it is the same
request `revision-log.md` has always made: **read the Protected Ledger before you fix anything.**
The flaws that look like mistakes are signed. An error that produces meaning when noticed is a
tremor; an error that produces none is noise.

If you change it, the license already requires you to say so. What the author asks on top of that
is smaller and not enforceable: don't hand someone a sanded copy and call it the original. Say
whose hand did what.

What the law still holds onto is the one thing that mattered most. Attribution is a condition of
this license, not a courtesy. Their names stay on their work.

And the book's own law is unchanged, because it never needed a license behind it: **when struck,
answer** — with your own telling, your own voice, your own work. The fifth shelf was always cut
for whoever comes next.
